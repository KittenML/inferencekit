# Consumer memory within latency and quality gates

Use with [optimizing-inference](../SKILL.md). Register `goals.affordability`: `memory_gb_max`, `memory_kind` (`vram`, `unified`, `host`), optional smaller `stretch_memory_gb_max`, optional host-memory cap and `offload_allowed` (default false). Save raw bytes and device capacity; GB is decimal. Set `goals.lead: affordability` to minimize memory, or attach the limits to a speed goal. Declare permitted numerics, a latency target and `quality.protocol` with quality bounds. Missing modality evidence is UNPROVEN.

`inferencekit bench image` and `bench audio` measure client timing and structural validity; `--help` lists flags. They require a running server and do not prove semantic quality. Images: POST `/v1/images/generations` with model, prompt, width/height, steps, guidance_scale, seed and settings; JSON response has image_b64, format=png, effective (including steps_run and requested settings), cache=[]. Speech: POST `/v1/audio/speech` with model, input, seed, sample_rate, channels, encoding=pcm_s16le, stream=true, settings; flushed NDJSON events are metadata (rate/channels/encoding), audio (pcm_b64), final (sample_count per channel, effective model/seed/settings). Pins must match effective values. First PCM, first nonzero arrival and simulated playback onset are distinct; silent samples do not prove speech. Use one round per fresh process and --extend per engine; lifecycle and quality need external proof.

1. Identify device/runtime with `doctor` and the [hardware guide](../../inference-triage/references/hardware.md). Unknown architecture, profiler, flag or workaround requires current official-doc retrieval, URL/date/version recorded, then a pinned smoke test. CUDA support does not establish HIP or Metal support.
2. Tune the baseline for **the same goal and gates**: supported precision, residency, allocation, compilation and batching. Retain its fastest feasible and minimum-memory feasible configurations. Comparing against an unnecessarily resident or untuned baseline cannot substantiate the claim.
3. Account for weights, activations/workspaces, KV/diffusion state, text encoder, VAE, codec/vocoder, cache/runtime and host staging. Record peak allocation/reservation, whole-device usage and host/process memory; these overlap, so do not sum them. Apple unified memory includes CPU/OS. Record pressure/swap; allocator bytes alone do not prove fit.
4. Test residency/lifetime changes, duplicate-copy removal, chunking/tiling and supported kernels. Authorized quantization/offload are ordinary candidates: record format/group size/excluded modules, residency schedule, transfers and resolved settings. Include transfer latency; memory wins missing latency or quality gates are infeasible. Declare numerics changed for lossy results.
5. Use the platform [timeline and supported kernel profiler](../../profiling/SKILL.md), Amdahl and mechanism evidence. Disable profiling for timed balanced fresh-process A/B rounds; pre-register samples and CI rules. One timing owner per device, with independent devices parallel only when authorized.

**Images:** pin prompts, resolution, scheduler/steps, guidance/negative prompts, seed/initial noise and output format. Client time ends at the final decoded image; loading/compile are separate. Evaluate adherence/layout/text, artifacts and perceptual quality against the agreed budget. PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS expose drift but alone do not prove generative quality. If fewer steps or precision changes are authorized, use `compare --image-tradeoff` for matched task hashes and report the timing as a format/work tradeoff with separate quality evidence; never claim unchanged work or quality from the timing verdict. Use an image adapter, never token/video latency or GSM8K proxies.

**Speech:** pin text/voice/language, reference audio, chunking and playback buffer. `ttfa_p95_ms` is request-to-first **playable audio**, including preprocessing/encoding/client buffering, not first codec token. Real-time speech also needs sustained RTF <=1 and no underruns on long samples. Register WER/CER, speaker similarity and artifact/prosody checks, margins, paired CIs and human review. Check streaming and final waveform on the exact speed configuration; token gates do not substitute.

Report a Pareto table: raw device/host bytes, p95 latency, sustained throughput, quality and verdict, baseline/candidate, evidence ids or immutable external hashes. Fit must hold through loading and worst-case generation. Label cold/warm/compile costs and preserve losses. A 24 GB run does not prove a 16 GB stretch. All memory, latency, quality and mechanism gates must hold on the requested hardware.

Explicit authorized `budget.spend_cap: none`, `stage_timeboxes: false`, `report_reserve_h: 0` override only the corresponding run defaults. Keep validation and packaging current continuously through its finite deadline.
